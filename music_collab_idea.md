# music collab idea

Not started. Written down so it isn't forgotten.

Idea for a relatively simple but also a little complicated app.
Wanted to make a simple app so I can make a song together with a
friend. Requires an entire UI and figuring out how to do DAW stuff
as well as logic for collaboration. The collab part should be easy
with yrs. The rest not very.

I need an entire UI as well as a separate project just to figure out
how to express "I have a note X, play it through a VST like Vital".
Ideally Vital and samples are the only instruments at first. Samples
for drums and sound effects or whatever, Vital for any instruments.
Just because it reduces complexity a lot and it's good enough to
make stuff sound good.

Also might need to use a different GUI thingy for this. Not sure
egui is very good for this. Or if I do it with egui, then I first
need to figure out how to optimize the app when there are loads of
objects on screen and stuff.

So that's kind of a lot.
